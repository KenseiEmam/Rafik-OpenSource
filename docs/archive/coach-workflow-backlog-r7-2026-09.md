## Priority recorded at the R7 checkpoint

R7 is an incremental daily-workspace checkpoint. **R7A1/R7A2 foundation is implemented locally:** Prisma/PostgreSQL API and separate membership/context/responsibility. Organization-owned delivery records continue in R8. See the [original-role audit](role-scope-audit-2026-09-23.md) and [ADR 0005](../decisions/ADR-0005-organization-and-independent-scopes.md). R8 then implements scoped programming/delivery; R9 coach services, organizational calendar/assessments and distinct analytics; R10 scoped notifications; R11 operational/release readiness and platform administration; R12 available review/refinement and actual-use evidence. Native remains last.

The user's missing-feature examples (analytics and optional program imagery) are incorporated below. Real participants and hardware cannot be simulated into completed gates; customer recruitment is not a prerequisite for implementing the known product improvements.

## Specific founder requests

- **Coach analytics:** informative diagrams for actual client activity, training completions, check-in response and review turnaround, with date ranges, denominators and drill-through. Do not call current-plan projections historical adherence or classify clients by invented scores.
- **Organization-admin analytics:** staffing/client distribution, enrollment trends, unassigned trainees, program participation, attendance and assessment completion/trends. These differ from coach analytics. Use the corrected organization-owned delivery model; organizational admin oversight must not leak independent or personal records.
- **Program imagery (suggestion, assessed):** optional cover imagery is useful for recognizing/browsing multiple programs, especially the later native catalog. It should accompany goal, duration, frequency, equipment and level, with consistent crops and an accessible no-image fallback. A photo alone is weak decision support; it is not a reason to build an unvalidated marketplace or expose private programs. Consider additional instructional images only at the exercise where they clarify movement. R8 owns metadata/cover design and its publication/storage lifecycle; R9 owns the distinct analytics experiences.
